## Connection should feel different

Most networks reward noise. We're exploring something more personal: an experience where **relevance, trust, and reputation** matter more than follower counts.

Pairty is designed around a simple belief: who you know matters, but **the quality of your connections matters more.**

### Discover · Connect · Build trust

- **Thoughtful discovery:** Find relevant people and communities with purpose.
- **Network-aware experiences:** Clear in-network and out-of-network connections.
- **Reputation over speculation:** Recognize genuine participation and trust, not price tickers.
- **Designed for people:** Premium, fast, and simple.

> Good connections create opportunity. Great connections create belonging.
